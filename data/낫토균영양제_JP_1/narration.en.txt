This is a supplement sold at Don Quijote in Osaka — instead of eating natto itself, you just take the fermenting bacteria, packed into a pill.

The gold box says "NATTO-KIN+ENZYME" in bold letters, with "45 billion natto bacteria" printed right underneath.

A lot of people can't stand the smell or the stickiness of real natto, so this brand pulled out just the fermented bacteria and turned it into tablets.

The label lists 45 billion natto bacteria, 18 billion lactic acid bacteria, and 1.8 billion butyric acid bacteria.

These are spore-forming bacteria that survive stomach acid, so they're said to reach your intestines alive.

One natto bacterium is said to multiply into 4 billion within 16 hours.

On top of that, they blended in pineapple, papaya, and kiwi enzymes — that's what sets this one apart.

The dosage itself is simple.

One tablet after every meal, four tablets a day as the standard.

Real reviews mention things like mild constipation improving and gas not building up as much.

But reviews for the same brand's other natto bacteria product are more mixed — some people say they don't feel any effect at all.

And since it's a natto-based fermented bacteria, the label warns that people on blood thinners like warfarin should avoid it.

Bottom line — NATTO-KIN+ENZYME skips the natto itself and gives you just the fermenting bacteria in pill form, which isn't something you'll usually find on a typical vitamin shelf, and the reviews on it are honestly a mixed bag.
