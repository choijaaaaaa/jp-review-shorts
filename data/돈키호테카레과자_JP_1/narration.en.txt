This is Samyang's Buldak Curry — sold at Don Quijote in Osaka, but you can't actually buy it in Korea.

The box has a huge spicy-level label, and people who've tried it react with things like "it's so spicy I basically froze."

It packs five spices — cumin, coriander seed, fenugreek, cardamom, and pepper — so after the heat hits, a burst of bold spice flavor follows.

That spicy-sweet combo is very Korean, and it's honestly the biggest draw of this curry.

On the spice scale, it's about twenty-five times spicier than Curry LEE, the mild curry everyone associates with "gentle."

Like Buldak fire noodles, it's addictive — you keep going back for more even while sweating through it.

Bottom line: the heat is genuinely Buldak-level, but with real spice depth behind it — if you like spicy food, this curry will not disappoint.
