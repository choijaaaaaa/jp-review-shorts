This collagen jelly was sitting on a Don Quijote shelf in Osaka — it's the brand's first-ever premium line in 15 years.

The package says "Collagen C Jelly PremiumRich," and each stick is packed with pink jelly inside.

This jelly series has been around since 2011, but this premium version just launched for the first time.

Beauty ingredients went from 7 types to 10, and they added a brand-new one called glucosylceramide.

One box has 15 sticks, and together they pack 37,500 milligrams of fish collagen, plus a new blend with 20% golden pineapple juice.

Reviews for this brand's collagen jelly line are honestly pretty mixed.

Some people say the berry flavor is sweet and tangy and easy to eat every day, while others love the chewy texture.

On the flip side, some reviewers said the gelatin taste is too strong and the artificial sweetener lingers afterward.

One more thing — the sister product released alongside it isn't even collagen, it's placenta jelly, so it's easy to grab the wrong one off the shelf.

Bottom line: this Collagen C Jelly PremiumRich got a real ingredient upgrade for its first premium release in 15 years, but whether you love the taste and texture comes down to personal preference.
