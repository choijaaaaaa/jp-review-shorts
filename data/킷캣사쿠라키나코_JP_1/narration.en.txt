I picked up this KitKat at Don Quijote in Osaka, and the flavor name threw me off — it's called Sakura Kinako, cherry blossom mixed with roasted soybean powder.

The box says "Sakura Kinako" in big letters, and Nestlé's own official account confirmed this flavor is exclusive to Don Quijote.

Kinako, if you haven't run into it before, is the toasted soybean powder you'll see dusted on mochi in Japan — nutty, slightly sweet, and a little smoky.

Flip the box over and it checks out: white chocolate dusted with sakura leaf powder, and actual kinako mixed right into the wafer cream.

Reviews on this one are pretty split. Some people say the kinako smell hits the moment they open the bag, exactly what they were hoping for.

Others say the sakura white chocolate is great, but the kinako flavor itself is fainter than expected.

This flavor first showed up around 2018, and it's kept coming back to Don Quijote shelves as a seasonal release for years since.

Bottom line: this Sakura Kinako KitKat is a Don Quijote-only find, and whether the kinako hits you strong or faint really depends on who you ask.
