This is the sake-scented KitKat you'll find at Don Quijote in Osaka — one bite in, and it actually tastes like real Japanese sake.

The box says "Sakura Nihonshu" in big letters, and people who've actually tried it are pretty split.

Some say the sake flavor hits immediately and the aroma is strong. Others say it's more like a dry, rougher sake smell.

But flip the box over and the alcohol content is just zero point zero nine percent. Japan's legal cutoff for something to count as alcohol is one percent, so this is officially just candy.

Bottom line: the sake scent is genuinely strong, but you won't get buzzed — it's a novelty candy you eat purely for the fun of it.

And just so you know, it's not an actual sake brewery collab. Nestlé blended the nihonshu flavor in-house.

So even though there's basically zero alcohol, the sake aroma is the real deal — a fun souvenir candy even if you can't drink.
