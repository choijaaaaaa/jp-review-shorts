This is KINCHO's mosquito spray from a Don Quijote shelf in Osaka — and the box actually says "never spray this on your body."

The instructions are just: press it once a day. That's it.

The idea is you spray it hard onto the ceiling and walls so the chemical sticks there, and any mosquito that lands on that spot drops on contact.

People say it's almost scary how few mosquitoes they see afterward, and some even say it still works with the windows open.

One spray lasts about twelve hours — press it once in the evening and it usually holds until morning.

A single can has enough left over to carry you into next summer.

Bottom line: it clearly works, just remember it's a room treatment — never spray it directly on people.
