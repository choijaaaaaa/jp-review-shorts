This suisai wash is sitting on the shelf at Don Quijote in Japan, and in just six months it's already sold enough to stack up 269 Tokyo Towers.

The box is stamped with "everyday pore care" in big letters, plus its name, paste-type enzyme wash, right alongside a rundown of the two enzymes and two micro-clays packed inside.

suisai actually got its start back in 2005, launched by Kanebo.

This year marks its 20th anniversary.

It's held the number one sales spot in Japan's face-wash market for eleven years running, and the original powder wash alone has sold more than thirty million units.

This paste wash is that same line's newest release, launched in September 2025.

Even so, within six months it picked up eleven Best Cosme awards, and shipments passed half a million units.

Stack up all those tubes, and you'd get roughly 269 Tokyo Towers.

Popular beauty YouTuber Hiro Odagiri posted a recommendation video, and that's what really got people talking.

Plenty of real reviews mention buying it specifically because they saw her video.

The formula runs on two enzymes, protease for protein and lipase for sebum, mixed with Moroccan lava clay and kaolin.

There are two ways to use it, too — work it into a foam, or just smooth it on and massage.

Reviewers are split: some say it doesn't get skin instantly smooth like the original powder wash, but plenty note it leaves skin moisturized with zero tightness.

Bottom line, this is suisai's name recognition plus a buzzy new launch — the suisai Beauty Clear Paste Wash leans more toward moisture than that classic smooth finish.
