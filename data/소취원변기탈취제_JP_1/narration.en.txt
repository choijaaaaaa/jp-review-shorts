Found this deodorizer at Don Quijote in Japan, and the whole trick is just one drop into the toilet water.

There were two scents sitting side by side on the shelf, matcha and sweet rose, and the bottle itself is about the size of eye drops.

It's simple to use: before or after you use the toilet, drop one or two drops right where the water sits.

That's genuinely it.

A 20-milliliter bottle is supposed to last about 640 uses at one drop each, which is pretty long-lasting for something this small.

Rakuten reviews say things like, "just one drop and the rose scent spreads — I also use spray and stand-type deodorizers, but this works best."

Someone else wrote that the green scent feels a bit stronger than rose, but the deodorizing power stays the same either way, and they can't be without it anymore.

Not every review is glowing, though. One person admitted it's surprisingly hard to measure out exactly one drop, but even a few drops kill the smell, so they keep buying it again.

Reactions got pretty intense too, with reviews like, "I've tried all kinds of air fresheners and they always felt too strong, but this one drop made the bathroom smell great — I wish I'd known about it sooner."

Bottom line: Kobayashi's Shoshugen — the deodorizer you drop straight into the toilet water — is a genuinely novel idea, and it's clearly why people keep coming back to buy it again.
